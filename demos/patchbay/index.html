Examples

otoji Patchbay music nodes in the pipeline language

Each line is a node. Edit the patch and it applies as you type; the column on the right is generated live and is never part of the source. Alt+T mutes or unmutes the line under the cursor.

stopped

Nothing plays until you press Start. Esc stops at any time; switching tabs stops too. Output goes through a limiter.

—
Alt+T mute/unmute line now Alt+E mute/unmute at the next bar Alt+B play/stop Esc stop

Nodes
clockbpm=92 sig=4 · environment: music nodes take the first one automatically
metro节拍器, accent on beat 1 · tone=1000
drumspattern="kick|snare|hat", 16 steps, x hit, X accent · with ^vad: kick|snare|hat, 1/16|1/8|1/4|off
micasks for the microphone on Start; input only, never played back
learnmic> learn beat 4beats|2bars|section · records from the next beat or bar, rewrites a drums (beatbox) or piano (hum) line, then turns itself off
vadthreshold=0.02 silence=300ms holdoff=250ms · other nodes react with ^vad ?vad !vad
pianochords="Am F C G" one per bar · style=arp|block oct=4 · or a melody notes="E4 . . G4"
basschords="…" roots on beats 1 and 3
strings pluck flute organ choir bellinstruments: phrase="5 - 6 7 1'" (简谱) with scale= key=, or chords; adsr= per note
scalemajor minor dorian phrygian … hijaz bayati rast (maqam) · in yo hirajoshi ryukyu · gong shang jue zhi yu yanyue
kit=drums: rock, darbuka, taiko, opera (京剧)
mixmixer bus: adds inputs into one signal
effectsfilter lp 800 drive chorus phaser tremolo 6Hz pan auto=0.2Hz eq low=3 comp -20 · wet= blends
env fadein fadeout包絡: env 50ms 2s ?vad, fadein 4bars, fadeout 2bars after=8bars
delaytime=3/16 feedback=0.35 wet=0.3, tempo-synced
reverbsize=2 wet=0.25 (or 2s)
speakervolume=0.5 · only what reaches a speaker is heard

Syntax
. drumsa source; the name defaults to the kind
drums,piano> mixinputs; spaces allowed, | optional
> speakerinput from the previous line
*> speakerevery fitting, not-yet-used output in this group (blank line or --- starts a group)
. metro2=metroa second node of a kind (unnamed ones become metro2)
clock2> metropick an environment instead of the first clock
#. metromuted; # text is a comment
^vadon: fire per onset (on=vad)
?vad !vadonly while / not while vad hears speech (while= / unless=)
. clock 100 3/4keys can go when the value is obvious, like CSS
