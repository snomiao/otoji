Mic settings

The installed userscript supplies your private settings form. Configuration and account tokens stay in your userscript manager's storage.

Waiting for otoji mic. If the settings form does not appear, install the userscript, enable it in your userscript manager, and reload this page.

The public page cannot read your private configuration or account tokens.

Developing locally? Install the loopback-only loader. It replaces the regular script; verify your manager retained your settings, and keep only one version enabled.
